About Us

What We're Building

We're creating an AI evaluation tool that tests how well language models handle ambiguity, context, and factual claims. When you submit a prompt and an AI response, our system outputs a reliability index, a score that tells you how well the AI handled the task. The tool is designed to catch specific failure modes: ethics, missing context, hallucinations, and more. It's built for researchers, educators, and anyone who needs to understand when AI responses are trustworthy.

Our mission

AI is powerful, but it's not perfect. Models can sound confident while making things up, miss important context, or give you incorrect citations. Our mission is to make those failures visible and measurable. By creating a rigorous evaluation framework, we're helping people understand AI limitations and helping AI builders improve.

About BranchOut! (Our Team)

BranchOut! is a student-led non-profit organization promoting STEM learning through fostering cultural understanding and civic engagement. Our goal is to inspire a passion for learning through connections between communities, creating and maintaining safe spaces for innovation, collaboration, and support. Regarding the BranchOut! Innovator program specifically, participants use their programming, design, and marketing skills in order to create an online application with a specific task to accomplish. This project-based program also is designed for participants to use their creativity skills to create this effective and unique project.
